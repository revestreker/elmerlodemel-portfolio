    Hidden Islands (TV-series) - OYA Studio

    I served as a storyboard artist on half the episodes of “Hidden Islands,” a 20-part animated family adventure series produced by OYA Copenhagen. The show follows three children stranded on a mythical island chain who must work together to unravel a world-saving secret.

    Episode 15

    Episode 19

    “I had the pleasure of directing Elmer at OYA Copenhagen. He worked as a storyboard and animatics artist on our first animated TV Show. He did 10 full episodes and left me impressed on a number of occasions.

    He knows his craft, he's talented and creative. He is adaptable but he has his own distinct style and look and cinematic flair, which I rate highly.

    He is also very willing to learn and improve and very good at taking constructive feedback and applying that feedback to his work.

    On a personal level, he is an allround nice guy! Kind, funny, humble, norwegian!

    I can only recommend Elmer and I really hope we get to work together many more times in the future!”

    Peter Egeberg Director, Hidden Islands — OYA Copenhagen
